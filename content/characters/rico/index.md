Rico is the original character of DrRaccoon and has been in existence since late 2008. He was originally born as a fursuit back on December 2008 where the fursuit creator, Jill0r, was given artistic freedom. He was created before even a ref sheet was created, as the character was formed to be whatever was fabricated as the fursuit.

## "Lore"

Rico Pinecoon has a Ph.D in Malpractice and can frequently be found toying around with new technology, chemistry, and other altering forces to find ways to toy with peoples appearance and other attributes. Some say when it comes to Chemistry, he cant be trusted. Be careful of what liquids he offers for you to drink or "try out".
Fursuit Details

## Fursuit

Ricos fursuit came to life in 2008, and the character stemmed off the Fursuit creation. The head was made by Jill0r with full artistic freedom given. Prior to the fursuit head, no artwork of Rico existed. Handpaws, Original Tail and Original Feed were made by SavageTurtleStudios. The new (larger) footpaws, 8 foot tail and arm sleeves made by Tamron.
