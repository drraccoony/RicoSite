Buttermilk the cow is an original fursuit & character made by "Stoof". Originally created sometime in 2018(?), "Stoof" only had worn the character out to a handful of events, and eventually retired the suit. At this time, "Stoof" made a mention of "retiring" buttermilk. I had reached out inquiring about her. Nearly 5-6 months later, I got a reply mentioning Buttermilk was still available.

This suit was acquired in February 2020 with no padding. 40lbs of Polyfill was purchased, which the suit used approx 20-25lbs of.

## Why?

I have always been a huge fan of large & oversized fursuits. I have, for the longest time, wanted to own a big thick plush fursuit. Eventually Mocha will be a big plush fursuit eventually created, but I figured Buttermilk would be a good alternative for the time being, Plus, getting to be a big thick oversized plush cow was something different and new.Add Buttermilk's bio here.
