Mocha is a "Red Kangaroo", however not your most average kangaroo standing at 10 feet tall. In some cases shes been seen larger than 10 feet, but said encounters have not been properly documented or recorded. She sports around a oversized raygun which has the ability to change ones size. Either making them larger or smaller. The effects are temporary... Most of the time. She has a fascination & irresistible urge to pouch people smaller than herself. If said person is too large for her pouch, that is generally when she is reaching for her raygun.

## Experimental iterations
Early experimental fabrications of Mocha were of a "Pooltoy" form before finally refining the characater to be more of a organic or plush character for durablity purposes. Sizes of Mocha as a pooltoy were unpredictable, with the largest documented size being 8x her original size (40ft). 

## Gender
Mocha has a pouch, however, is not identified by any gender. Though sometimes the pro-noun "she" is used a lot since female kangaroos are the only ones with pouches. Instead of referring to her as "it" or "mocha" from a third person perspective, saying "She" or "Her" is easier. Though she still remains Androgyne.
