In March 2022, Valve released the Recovery Image for the SteamDeck. However, My steamdeck reservation was still not available until Q2 of the year for me. So I figured why not take my existing GPD Win 2, and load something onto it for fun. In summary, We took the Recovery Image, tweaked the shell scripts for reimaging, rolled back gamescope, and the intel drivers.

## Tweaking
After 4 straight days of tinkering and working with the GPD community and a few other people doing the same thing, I got SteamOS3 working quite flawlessly on the GPD Win 2. I was even surprised to see the docking working with a external keyboard, mouse and HDMI monitor. I could game over this without any second guesses that I was actually running it on a GPD Win 2.
