I've led the video department for Furry Migration since 2017 — an outreach event run by MNFurs, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. The goal has always been simple: capture the moment and get it in front of people who couldn't make it to the con. What started as basic SD-card recording grew year over year into full livestreaming by 2019, covering main-stage events like dance competitions with proper multi-camera coverage.

By 2019 the setup supported simultaneous recording and livestreaming through vMix — multi-camera switching, lower-thirds, video rolls, ad/sponsor blocks — with audio handled by the con's Main Stage A/V team, and some experimental deployment to digital signage around the venue.
