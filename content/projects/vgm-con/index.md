Ran online production for VGM Con 2022, an annual video game music convention in Minneapolis, managing a dedicated online stage that broadcast to Twitch. The setup mixed remote performer streams with pre-recorded content from a control room built around vMix, three SDI camera inputs via Blackmagic DeckLink cards, and an AKAI APC Mini for tactile control.

Remote performers streamed in over cloud-based RTMP servers, coordinated through Discord, alongside local acts. Connection quality varied enough among remote performers to cause some visible degradation on stream, which fed directly into planning for the following year — better rate-control settings, newer protocols like SRT and NDI, and redundant backups.
