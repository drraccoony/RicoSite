Tennant is a lightweight, browser-based timecard app built as an alternative to spreadsheets or expensive enterprise tools for tracking volunteer and small-business hours. Most of the effort went into the frontend — Bulma for the UI, Express-Handlebars for templating — on top of a Node.js/MySQL backend, deployed to Heroku with environment-based config for credentials.

User accounts and authentication were originally planned but session-management issues kept that scope from getting finished. Reporting, permission-based accounts, email activation, and password recovery are all still on the wishlist.
