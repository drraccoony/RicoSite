I put together custom livestream production assets for friends and other content creators — overlays, standby screens, and roll-in videos themed around whatever game they're playing. Work has covered Minecraft, Sea of Thieves, and Elite Dangerous so far, ranging from generic templates to fully customized branding per streamer.

Assets typically include startup screens, end cards, and cinematic transitions timed to music, with an emphasis on making the stream feel intentional rather than default-OBS.
