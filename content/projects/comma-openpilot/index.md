In 2021, a friend in Texas showed off his CommaAi device — an open-source entry into level-2 driver assistance. He set me up with his old unit since he'd moved on to a Tesla. I brought it back to Minnesota, picked up a Honda Harness Kit, and it worked right out of the gate in my 2019 Honda HR-V. It's a neat piece of hardware once you realize it's really just a LeEco Le Pro3 cellphone repurposed as a dev kit.

In February 2022 the device froze mid-drive and never came back — bricked, as far as I could tell after a fair bit of struggling with re-flashing instructions for a fairly non-standard phone. About a month later I rebuilt it with a refurbished LeEco Le Pro3 from a community member, swapped it into the original enclosure, and got it running again — though it initially refused to detect the car at all. Turned out to be a bad USB-C cable, not the device.
