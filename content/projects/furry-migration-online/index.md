When the pandemic shut down in-person events, we put together Furry Migration Online — a free convention alternative streamed on New Year's Eve 2020, with live hosts, panels, DJ sets, and guest speakers. We picked Mux as the streaming platform to keep full control over the player and viewing environment, and to properly respect the ASCAP/BMI music licensing we held.

Production ran on a vMix HD license for multi-camera mixing, with animated transitions, lower-thirds, live social feeds, and pre-recorded segments (panels, roll-ins, donation incentives) mixed in alongside live content — including three DJs streaming in over RTMP and a VRChat panel. Audio ran through a USB audioboard with a mix of shotgun and lavalier mics.
